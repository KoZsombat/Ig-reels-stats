How do I get my data?
  1. In Instagram open Settings → Accounts Center → Your information and permissions → Download your information.
  2. Choose Some of your information and select Ads information (that's where watch history lives) — or just export everything.
  3. Pick Download to device, Date range All time, and Format: JSON (not HTML).
  4. When Instagram emails you that it's ready, download the .zip and drop it above.

Instagram stores watched videos in videos_watched.json. It doesn't separate Reels from other videos, and it may only keep a limited recent window, so treat the numbers as a close estimate.
